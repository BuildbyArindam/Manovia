[preface / curated]
Before we begin, a quick note. This is a gentle, self-guided practice, not medical advice, and it is not treatment. You can stop at any time; there is nothing to finish, and nothing here will continue on its own. If at any point it feels unwell, simply stop and do what feels right for you.

[stop / curated]
You can stop at any time, and you can let go of any instruction that does not suit you.

[eyes / curated]
You can keep your eyes open if you like, resting your gaze somewhere quiet in front of you.

[opening / curated]
You do not need to feel calmer for this to be worth it. It is enough to be here, breathing, for a few minutes.

[passage / curated]
Notice the sounds around you. Far and near, coming and going. You do not need to identify them. Let them be the sound of the place where you are.

[passage / curated]
Thoughts will come, and that is fine. You do not need to push them away or follow them. Just notice, and let the breath keep going.

[breath / curated]
Notice one breath at a time. In, and out. You are welcome to let your attention wander, and you are welcome back to the breath when it comes.

[passage / curated]
Notice the weight of your body where it is supported. The chair, the floor, the ground. You do not have to hold yourself up this minute.

[passage / curated]
If you can, let the breathing be a little slower than it needs to be. Not forced just allowed. In, and out, at the speed that feels right.

[breath / curated]
For the next few breaths, let the breath out be a little longer than the breath in, if that feels comfortable. If not, just breathe as you are.

[closing / curated]
You can return to the day whenever you are ready. Take one more breath in, and out and let the rest of this stay with you if you want it to. You can stop now, whenever you are ready.
