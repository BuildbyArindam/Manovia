## What could not be measured here

Every setting in the harness was measurable in this environment.

- No torch, transformers or sentence-transformers in this sandbox (Hugging Face is unreachable), so no multilingual *checkpoint* row: the fine-tuned dense row is the offline contrastive projection, and the cross-encoder row is the distilled lexical scorer. `ml/notebooks/colab_retrieval_finetune.ipynb` runs the same loss on a real checkpoint and on a real cross-encoder, on the pairs and examples these stages wrote.
- Which also means the `*_finetuned` rows are **not servable today**: the backend's embedder factory builds `tfidf`, `hashing` or `sentence_transformers`, not an ml projection matrix. They measure what contrastive training does to this corpus in the only space this sandbox can train in, and they are the ablation a checkpoint run is compared against. What ships is `RETRIEVER=hybrid_rerank` with `RAG_EMBEDDER=tfidf`, which is why the recommendation is chosen among the settings that have both a calibrated abstain threshold and a servable encoder.
