Self-help techniques are real tools, and they carry a lot. But they are not
meant to carry everything, forever. Knowing when to bring in a professional
is not admitting defeat — it is using the right tool for the size of the
job.

Some signals that it may be time:

- **Weeks, not days.** The heaviness, worry, sleeplessness or emptiness has
  been with you most days for a few weeks, not lifting after rest or a good
  weekend.
- **It is costing you things you value.** Your studies, your friendships,
  your appetite, your sleep, the activities that used to be yours.
- **You are working hard and nothing shifts.** You have tried the routines,
  the breathing, the talking — and you are still sinking.
- **The effort of pretending is becoming its own job.**
- **Alcohol, avoidance, isolation or endless scrolling** have quietly become
  how you get through most days.
- **Your thoughts turn dark** — hopelessness that does not lift, or any
  thought of harming yourself. This one does not wait for weeks: please tell
  someone today. Use the help button in this app for a helpline, contact a
  doctor, or, if you are in immediate danger, your local emergency number.
  You deserve company for this, right now.

Going early is allowed. You do not need to be at your worst to deserve
support, and you do not need a "big enough" reason. "I've been struggling
for a while and I'd like help" is a complete sentence.

If the first professional is not a good fit, that is a match problem, not a
you problem. It is okay to try again with someone else.
