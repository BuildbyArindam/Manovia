Box breathing gives your mind a simple square to trace while your body
settles. It is popular with people who like structure, and you can do it with
your eyes open, mid-task.

The square:

1. Breathe in for a count of **4**.
2. Hold, lightly, for a count of **4**.
3. Breathe out for a count of **4**.
4. Rest for a count of **4** — then begin again.

Draw the square in your mind's eye as you go: one side per count. Some people
trace it on their leg with a finger instead.

Keep the holds soft. If holding your breath feels uncomfortable, shorten it
to 2, or skip the hold and simply breathe in 4 and out 4. The counts serve
you, not the other way round.

Three or four boxes are enough for a quick reset. A few minutes is a longer
rest for your nervous system.

If you feel dizzy or air-hungry, stop counting and breathe naturally. This is
meant to be one of the easiest things you do today.
