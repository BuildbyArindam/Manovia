When you are tense, your breath tends to get short and high in your chest,
and your body reads that as more reason to be tense. Slowing the breath out
is one of the simplest ways to tell your body the moment is passable.

Try this for a minute or two:

- Breathe in gently through your nose for a count of **four**.
- Breathe out through your mouth for a count of **six**, slow and easy, as if
  cooling soup.
- Pause for a beat, then repeat. Keep it comfortable — no straining, no big
  gulps of air.

If counting feels fussy, just make the out-breath noticeably longer than the
in-breath and let the pace be lazy.

A few gentle notes: it is normal to feel odd or slightly light-headed at
first — breathe normally if that happens. And the aim is not to feel nothing;
it is to take the edge off enough to think.

Four or five slow cycles is often enough to feel a little more settled.
Practising a couple of minutes a day when you are calm makes the skill easier
to use when you need it.
