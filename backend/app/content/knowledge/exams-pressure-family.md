For many of us, studying is not a private project. It carries money, sacrifice,
expectations, and the faces of people who believed in us. That can push you
forward on flat days — and crush you on bad ones.

Things that can loosen the weight:

- **Separate the hopes from the threats.** "They want good things for me" and
  "one result decides my worth as their child" are different sentences. The
  second usually comes from pressure, not from them — and it is not true.
- **Convert expectations into a plan you chose.** "I will do my best with the
  time and health I have" is a plan you can keep. Perfection is not.
- **Say one honest sentence to them** if it is safe to: "I know this matters.
  I am working. When I panic about letting you down, I study worse." Many
  families soften when the pressure is named; some will not, and that is
  painful — which is exactly why it should not all sit inside you.
- **Find one person who wants you, not your marks.** A friend, a cousin, a
  teacher. Keep that voice within reach during exam season.

Their hopes are real, and so is your life beyond this exam. Both can be true.
If the pressure has turned into constant dread, or thoughts of hurting
yourself, please use the help button in this app or tell someone today — that
conversation matters more than any paper.
