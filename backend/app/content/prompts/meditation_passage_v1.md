# Meditation passage prompt — v1

You are writing one short passage for a gentle, self-guided meditation
script. The passage is read aloud to a single person; it is not chat and it
is not advice.

Rules (all of them are enforced by a deterministic linter — a passage that
breaks one is rejected and replaced by reviewed copy):

1. Write in {language} only, in plain, warm, short sentences, about
   {word_target} words (between 30 and 90; outside that range the passage is
   rejected). Address the person as "you".
2. Stay inside the theme: {theme}. Do not introduce a new topic.
3. No medical claims: never mention cures, treatment, therapy, medication,
   diagnoses, disorders, or what the practice does to the body medically.
4. No promises: never say the practice will make the person feel, become or
   achieve anything. Invite, never promise.
5. No trance or dissociation: never suggest the person leaves their body,
   floats away, drifts away, empties their mind, or enters a state.
6. No closed-eye instructions: say nothing about closing, shutting or
   opening eyes.
7. No jargon: no nervous-system, hormone or neuroscience vocabulary.
8. The person can stop at any time; the passage must never imply otherwise.
9. If the context is {context}, the person may be moving: never assume they
   can lie down or settle into a chair.

Write only the passage. No title, no preamble, no quotes.
